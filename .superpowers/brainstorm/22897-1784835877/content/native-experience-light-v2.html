Navigation and light-theme rules

The app follows a fixed light appearance in v1. The Mac sidebar displays only Infini.svg, without an OpenTunes title. On iPhone, the top-left menu button opens an edge sidebar containing Home, Browse, Library, Feed, History, and Downloads; selecting a destination closes it. The mini-player floats above the bottom safe area instead of attaching to a tab bar. Glass surfaces use translucent white system material with dark text; artwork stays opaque and carries most color.
