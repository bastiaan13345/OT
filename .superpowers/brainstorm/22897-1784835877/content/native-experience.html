Interaction rules

Track, artist, playlist, history, feed, and search open in native navigation. Playback persists across sections. The queue opens only when requested. Tapping the iPhone mini-player presents full Now Playing; macOS exposes the queue as an inspector and can detach the optional always-on-top panel. Standard lists, forms, alerts, sliders, and download controls remain native and unglassed.
