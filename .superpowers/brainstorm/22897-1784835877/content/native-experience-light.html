Light-theme rules

The app follows a fixed light appearance in v1. Infini.svg is the primary app mark on launch, sidebar, and iPhone Home. Glass surfaces use translucent white system material with dark text; artwork remains opaque and carries most color. Rose is reserved for selection and playback state. Forms, lists, alerts, sliders, and download controls stay standard and high-contrast.
